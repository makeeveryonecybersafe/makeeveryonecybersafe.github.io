Before securing your accounts, you must accept a hard truth: Your data is likely already public.

When services like LinkedIn or Adobe are breached, hackers aggregate the data into "Collections." They use automated scripts to test these email/password combinations against other sites (Netflix, Amazon, Banking).

The Entropy Math

Length > Complexity

Most people think "Tr0ub4dor&3" is a strong password. Mathematically, it is not.

Computers guess passwords by crunching "Bits of Entropy."

  • Short & Complex (8 chars): ~40 bits of entropy. Cracked in hours.
  • Long & Simple (20 chars): ~100 bits of entropy. Takes millennia.

Mission: Create a password with > 100 years crack time using a "Passphrase" (e.g., 4 random words strung together).

The Vault Strategy

Zero-Memory Security

The human brain cannot remember 50 unique, high-entropy passwords. If you try, you will inevitably reuse them.

The solution is a Password Manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, etc.). You memorize ONE master passphrase (from Phase 2), and the Vault generates random noise for everything else.

Multi-Factor Armor

Moving Beyond SMS

Text Message (SMS) codes can be intercepted via "SIM Swapping." The standard is App-Based TOTP (Time-Based One-Time Password), like Google Authenticator or Authy.

Crucial Step: Backup Codes

If you lose your phone, these codes are your ONLY way back in. Treat them like cash.

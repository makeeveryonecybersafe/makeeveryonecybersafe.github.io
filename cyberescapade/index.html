The briefing

Open Source Intelligence — OSINT — is the practice of building a complete picture of a target using only what's already public. No exploits, no breaking in. Just knowing where to look, and how to read what you find.

CyberEscapade2026 puts that skill to the test. Your playground is two real, live websites. Hidden across them — in plain sight, in old pages, in things nobody thought to hide — are fragments of a flag. You get one clue per fragment. What you do with it is up to you.

Before the clock starts, you'll sit through a short session on how easily people are fooled online, followed by an hour of hands-on OSINT training. By the time the CTF begins, you'll already be seeing things you didn't notice an hour earlier.

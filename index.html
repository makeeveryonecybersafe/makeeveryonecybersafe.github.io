/ / About The Initiative

Beyond Presentations and Theory

MakeEveryoneCyberSafe (MECS) is a non-profit initiative focused on making cybersecurity awareness and education more engaging, accessible, and experience-driven.

We believe cybersecurity education should go beyond conventional lectures. Through interactive challenges, hands-on experiences, and collaborative campus initiatives, MECS aims to make cybersecurity relevant to everyone, regardless of their technical background.

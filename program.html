Digital Footprint & OSINT Foundations

Safely identify non-intrusive vulnerabilities such as open directories, public file exposure, and common web misconfigurations with ethical practices.

Deliverable: Vulnerability Observation Report
03

Social Engineering & Phishing Analysis

Analyze real-world scams and attack strategies. Understand the psychological triggers used in social engineering and how to prevent them.

Deliverable: Scam & Phishing Analysis Report
04

Cybersecurity Awareness & Final Project

Master the art of communicating security to non-technical users. Choose between creating awareness content, case studies, or a hardening guide.

Deliverable: Final Project Submission

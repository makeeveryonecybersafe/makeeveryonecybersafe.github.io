// WHAT WOULD HAVE STOPPED THIS
// VERIFY THE FULL SENDER DOMAIN
Display names are trivially spoofed. "CMS Platform Team" means nothing — the domain is the only signal. cms-mecs-platform.com is not a real MECS domain. Always expand the from field before clicking anything.
// REDUCE OSINT SURFACE
Jeffery's email, morning routine, CMS migration, tools used — all public. Attackers use this to craft personalized phishing that bypasses generic filters. Audit what you and your team share online.
// HARDWARE 2FA ON ALL ACCOUNTS
A captured password with hardware 2FA is worthless. The attacker cannot proceed without the physical token. This single control stops credential phishing entirely at the access stage.
// IMMUTABLE OFFSITE BACKUPS
Ransomware only wins when it destroys your only copy. Offsite, immutable backups mean you restore and move on without paying. All 31 MECS files were lost in 47 seconds.
// WRITTEN INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAN
When the ransom note appeared, MECS had no documented plan. Who do you call? Do you pay? How do you notify donors? Every org needs this written, tested, and ready before the attack happens.

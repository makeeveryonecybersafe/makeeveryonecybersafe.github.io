// UNRESTRICTED CREATIVITY

Build Anything That Protects Humans

Terms & Conditions simplifiers were just an example. You can build, design, or vibe-code anything that brings everyday users one step closer to being safe by default.

Anti-Phishing & Scam Shields

Tools, browser extensions, or link decloakers that instantly spot malicious URLs, fake sender handles, or urgent social engineering traps before a user clicks.

Dark Pattern Killers

Mechanisms that expose hidden subscriptions, bypass 5-step account cancellations, auto-opt out of shady data-sharing checkboxes, or neutralize fake countdown timers.

Background Permission Monitors

Lightweight app monitors or visual indicators that show real-time alerts when a service silently accesses clipboard data, location, camera, or background trackers.

Plain-English Translators

UI overlays or AI-powered wrappers that instantly distill complex legal policies or obscure privacy terms into 3-second nutrition labels users can actually digest.

AI Vibe-Coded Prototypes

Used Cursor, Bolt, or v0? Build rapid prototypes, web apps, or interactive safety widgets and submit your working demo or video walkthrough.

UI/UX Concept Designs

Not a hardcore coder? Submit high-impact Figma redesigns, wireframes, or graphic carousels showing how modern apps should look when built safe by default.

// CLARITY & DETAILS

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate?

Everyone! Students, professional developers, designers, security buffs, and AI vibe-coders from any stream or background can join individually or in teams.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. UI/UX concepts, Figma redesigns, wireframes and graphic carousels are welcome alongside working prototypes.

What can I submit?

Anything that brings everyday users closer to being safe by default: a repo, a live demo, a video walkthrough, or a design link.

How do I submit my project?

Click the "Register / Submit" button to fill out our Google Form with your details, project repository, video demo, or Figma design link.

Are there prizes?

Yes. Standout entries receive exciting prizes and recognition from MECS. Details will be shared with participants.

Is posting on Instagram or LinkedIn mandatory?

No, it is completely optional! However, sharing your progress or final build with #HackTheHuman2026 and tagging @MakeEveryoneCyberSafe helps build community hype and gets your work noticed early.

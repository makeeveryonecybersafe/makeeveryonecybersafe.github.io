Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the CyberEscapade2026 OSINT CTF event rules, eligibility, tools, and prerequisites.

CyberEscapade2026 is an OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) Capture The Flag (CTF) event. Participants are tasked with uncovering a final flag that has been fragmented across 2 live target websites within a fixed timeframe.
Participation is exclusively open to students of PSG College of Technology (PSG Tech). Valid student credentials will be verified during venue check-in.
This competition tests keen observational and analytical OSINT skills.
  • No Hacking Tools: Automated scanners, exploits, or penetration testing tools are strictly prohibited.
  • No Google Dorking: Search engine operators (Google dorks) are strictly prohibited.
  • Pure Observation: Participants must inspect and analyze the live web pages directly using basic web tools (e.g., standard browser capabilities and careful observation).
Teams must consist of exactly 2 participants. Only one registration per team is required—the team leader submits details for both members.
No, registration is completely free for all eligible PSG Tech students.
Each team must bring:
  • Laptop with Active Internet Connectivity (WiFi or mobile hotspot).
  • Paper and Pen (Required for taking notes during the pre-CTF briefing & training session).
